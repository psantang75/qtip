# Sales QA and SMB sales KB gap analysis

Reviewed 2026-09-14. **The largest gap is retrieval and consistency, not a lack of sales documentation.** The read-only inventory returned 891 accessible page records; all 179 pages in the Account Executive book and 40 other relevant pages were fetched successfully. This is not a claim to have read every page, attachment, external link or restricted book. “Not found” below means not found in that accessible inventory and relevant content review.

The missed-opportunities reviewer is configured with just six anchors: objection handling, ARP, one-call closing, closing tips, SXM inbound and SYB inbound. It fetches those pages' text, not their linked branches. An inbound flow linking to a warranty or discovery page does not give the AI that page's content.

This document is a proposed KB work list. No BookStack pages or company policies were changed. The [enhancement specification](sales_missed_opportunities_enhancement_plan.md) explains retrieval, metrics and rollout; the [play audit](sales_plays_audit.md) covers the current play library.

### Keep the publishing workload controlled

Start with Q1, Q3, Q4, Q7 and B2 as **five compact new pages/indexes**, plus targeted updates to existing pages. Put B8 inside Q7; put B1/B3/B4 examples into existing flows. Q6 is an HR clarification, not a new sales playbook. Add B5/B7/B10 only after the first release is working. Each policy needs one owner, last-reviewed date, applicability, exceptions, linked source and two or three examples; do not build another encyclopedia.

## 4. External benchmark basis and limits

- Gong's discovery research recommends targeted questions and reports an 11–14 question range and 46:54 seller/buyer talk ratio for its discovery population. These are contextual references, not requirements for every QTIP interaction. [Gong discovery research](https://www.gong.io/blog/discovery-call)
- Its newer analysis of 326,000 calls lasting at least ten minutes reports 57% seller talk on won deals versus 62% on lost deals; more questions did not automatically mean better outcomes. Do not turn the older 43:57 figure into a universal quota. [Gong updated talk-ratio research](https://www.gong.io/blog/talk-to-listen-conversion-ratio)
- Gong distinguishes question rate, talk ratio and interactivity rather than treating one count as sales quality. QTIP should publish its own precise metric definitions and avoid claiming formula equivalence. [Gong team-performance definitions](https://help.gong.io/docs/analyze-team-performance)
- Huthwaite's Quick Value Selling explicitly targets shorter selling cycles of three interactions or fewer. That is a useful fit for this team's brief consultative conversations; the proposed Dynamic Media workflow is our adaptation, not a certified implementation or promised uplift. [Huthwaite Quick Value Selling](https://www.huthwaiteinternational.com/sales-training/quick-value-selling)
